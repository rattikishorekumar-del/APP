Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Abroad Jobs app ("the App") collects, how it is used, and how it is protected. Abroad Jobs is a recruitment portal that helps candidates and recruitment associates connect around overseas job openings, and lets selected candidates check their placement status.

Information We Collect

When you register as a Candidate or Associate, we collect the information you provide directly, which may include:

  • Full name (used as your login ID)
  • Phone number(s) and WhatsApp number(s)
  • Email address (optional)
  • Trade / designation, work experience, and abroad-work experience
  • Age, state/location, and government ID type & number (Associates), or passport number (Candidates, optional)
  • For Associates: office name, address, branch locations, and trades offered
  • A password, which is never stored in plain text — only a one-way cryptographic hash is saved

If you use the "Know Your Status" search, you submit a passport number, which is used solely to look up your own selection/placement record.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and verify your login
  • To show you relevant overseas job requirements ("Hot Jobs")
  • To let recruiters/admins contact you directly via the phone and WhatsApp numbers you provide
  • To let you check your own selection, visa, and travel status by passport number
  • To prevent duplicate registrations (we check that a phone number or full name isn't already registered)

How Your Data Is Stored

App data is stored in a private Google Sheets spreadsheet and Google Drive folder controlled by the app administrator, accessed only through a secured Google Apps Script backend over HTTPS. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers or unrelated third parties. Data may be visible to the app administrator and authorized recruitment associates for the purpose of processing job placements.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your registration data is retained as long as needed to support the recruitment process. You can request deletion of your account and personal data at any time, in either of these ways:

  • In the app: log in, then tap the 🗑️ icon in the top header and submit the request form.
  • Online, without the app: visit our account deletion page and submit the same request form.

Deletion requests are reviewed and completed within 7 business days. We may contact you at the phone number you provide to verify your identity before deleting your data.

Children's Privacy

This app is intended for job seekers of legal working age and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Permissions the App May Use

The app may ask to open your phone's dialer or WhatsApp when you tap a Call or WhatsApp button — this simply hands off to those apps and does not give Abroad Jobs any additional access to your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
